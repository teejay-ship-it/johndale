Estimating Euroform materials by hand

A step-by-step standard operating procedure for newly hired office engineers on concrete construction projects.

Purpose

This procedure gives newly hired office engineers a systematic, manual method for estimating the Euroform materials needed for concrete works. It aims to produce accurate quantities, minimize wastage, and avoid material shortages or excess orders.

Scope

The procedure covers manual estimation of Euroform materials for:

  • Concrete columns
  • Concrete walls
  • Concrete beams
  • Concrete slabs
  • Other structural concrete elements requiring Euroform formwork systems

Step-by-step estimation process

1

Review the construction drawings

  • Obtain the latest approved structural and architectural drawings.
  • Identify the concrete elements requiring Euroform.
  • Check each element’s length, width, height, and thickness.
  • Identify the concrete pouring arrangement and construction joints.
  • Check for openings, beams, columns, intersections, and other obstructions.
  • Highlight all areas requiring formwork.
Important: Always use the latest approved drawings. Do not estimate using outdated or superseded plans.
2

Identify the Euroform panel sizes

Review the available Euroform panel inventory or the manufacturer’s catalog.

Open the Euroform catalog
3

Calculate the required formwork area

Calculate the surface area of each concrete element that requires formwork.

4

Convert formwork area into panel quantities

After calculating the total formwork area, determine the number of panels required.

Important: This is a preliminary, area-based estimate only. Determine the actual panel quantity by arranging the panels to the concrete dimensions, including edge panels, corners, tie locations, and panel orientation.
5

Estimate the required Euroform accessories

Besides panels, identify the accessories required by the actual Euroform system. Typical materials:

MaterialPurpose
Euroform panelsMain formwork surface
Wedge pinsConnect adjacent panels
Flat tiesResist concrete pressure
Hooks (short and long)Provide alignment and stability
Outside and inside cornersForm external and internal corners
Formwork oilPrevent concrete adhesion
Important: Do not estimate accessory quantities using arbitrary ratios. Follow the manufacturer’s system layout, allowable spacing, concrete pressure requirements, and project-specific engineering details.
6

Prepare the material summary

Consolidate the calculated materials into one material take-off (MTO).

7

Apply material allowance

Consider the following before finalizing the estimate:

  • Panel cutting and dimensional adjustments
  • Damaged or unusable materials
  • Project-specific construction requirements
  • Reuse and rotation of panels
  • Material availability at the project site

Do not automatically add a fixed percentage of wastage to reusable Euroform panels. Set allowances based on the actual panel layout and company policy.

8

Check and verify the estimate

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using outdated structural drawings.
  • Forgetting to account for both faces of concrete walls.
  • Including the top surface of slabs when it does not require formwork.
  • Forgetting beam soffits.
  • Using only total square meters to determine the final panel quantity.
  • Forgetting corner panels and connecting accessories.
  • Ignoring panel reuse and available site inventory.
  • Ordering materials without checking the actual formwork arrangement.
  • Failing to coordinate with the site engineer.

Recommended training workflow 5 days

Day 1

Drawing familiarization

Read structural plans, sections, elevations, and dimensions.

Day 2

Basic formwork computation

Practice formwork areas for columns, walls, beams, and slabs.

Day 3

Panel identification

Learn panel sizes, accessories, and actual material inventory.

Day 4

Manual material take-off

Do actual panel layout and accessory estimation on sample drawings.

Day 5

Actual project estimation

Prepare a complete MTO for a small project area, for senior engineer checking.

Final output required

Every completed Euroform material estimate must contain:

  • Project name and location
  • Drawing references and revision numbers
  • Formwork area computation
  • Euroform panel breakdown by size
  • Accessory and support material breakdown
  • Material reuse and allowance considerations
  • Total estimated material quantities
  • Prepared by, checked by, and approved by

Calculate the formwork area first, arrange the actual panels second, and estimate the accessories last.

This prevents the inaccurate estimates that come from simply dividing the total concrete formwork area by the panel area.
